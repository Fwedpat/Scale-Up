# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Scale-up founders and CEOs who need experienced guidance to turn growth ambitions into repeatable execution. They should understand the offer quickly and book a free consultation.

## Product Purpose

Scale-Up Navigator provides mentoring and advisory services for scale-up SMEs, helping leaders improve operations, sales excellence, and scale-up strategy.

## Positioning

The offer is grounded in 25+ years of frontline sales, marketing, and executive leadership across technology and software businesses, with practical guidance from strategy through execution.

## Operating Context

This is a single-page marketing site for prospective clients evaluating an independent advisor. Visitors may arrive from referrals, professional networks, or LinkedIn and need a clear path from credibility to contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first version is a static site suitable for GitHub Pages. The primary action uses a temporary `mailto:` destination until a booking URL is supplied. The page should work without JavaScript for core navigation and remain usable on small screens.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Scale-Up Navigator name, Michael Patterson attribution, existing logo, founder imagery, and video when supplied. Retain the current service language while correcting obvious copy errors and tightening the reading order.

## Evidence on Hand

The live site at `https://www.scaleupnavigator.com/` contains the current service descriptions, founder positioning, logo, imagery, and a background video. The user will provide replacement video and any final local assets before launch.

## Product Principles

- Make the value of experienced guidance obvious within the first viewport.
- Connect strategic advice to practical operating outcomes.
- Use evidence and specific service language instead of generic consulting claims.
- Give founders one clear, low-friction next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alternatives, and controls that remain understandable without color or animation.
